The Summit

The Leadership Conversation Procurement Needs Now

Procurement has moved far beyond sourcing and cost control. Today, it sits at the centre of business resilience, digital transformation, sustainability, enterprise value and growth.

The CIPS International Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027 brings together the leaders driving this transformation — creating one focused platform for insight, collaboration and meaningful industry dialogue.

What’s In It for You

Gain the insights, connections and perspectives you need to lead procurement with greater intelligence, resilience and value.

  1. 01

    The event is intended for senior procurement and supply chain leaders, providing opportunities for strategic networking, collaboration and knowledge sharing.

  2. 02

    Gain practical insights on AI, resilience, sourcing, sustainability and transformation.

  3. 03

    Connect with CPOs and decision-makers from India and international markets.

  4. 04

    Discover new ideas, solutions and approaches that can be applied within your organisation.

  5. 05

    Benchmark with peers facing similar procurement and supply chain challenges.

  6. 06

    Build high-value professional relationships across industries and markets.

  7. 07

    Take back actionable perspectives to improve strategy, performance and business impact.
