01Who we are

This website is operated for the CIPS International Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027 (the “Summit”) by the Summit organising team (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect through www.cips2027.online, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

02Information we collect

When you complete the registration form we collect:

  • Personal information: first name, last name, official email address, mobile number, country and (optionally) city.
  • Professional information: organisation or company and designation.
  • Your choices: that you wish to attend the Summit, and whether you agreed to receive event-related communication.
  • Submission details: the date and time of your submission.

We do not ask for payment card details, government identity numbers or any sensitive personal data through this website.

03How we use your information

  • To receive, review and manage your registration and to contact you about your participation.
  • To plan and run the Summit, including attendee communication, access and logistics.
  • To send you event-related updates about the Summit, only if you ticked the consent box. You can withdraw this at any time.
  • To keep the Summit secure, prevent misuse of the form and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

04Legal basis and consent

We process your registration details to take the steps you request when you register, and, where you have ticked the consent box, on the basis of your consent to receive event communication. Where applicable law requires a specific basis (for example the EU/UK GDPR or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), we rely on the basis that applies to your circumstances.

05Who we share it with

Your information is stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet, so Google acts as a service provider that hosts it on our behalf. Access is limited to the Summit organising team and authorised people supporting the Summit, such as CIPS colleagues and event service providers, and only to the extent needed to run the event. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

06Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It uses the system typeface already installed on your device, so no font files are requested from any third party. Registration data is sent only when you submit the form.

07International transfers

The Summit brings together delegates from India and other countries, and our service providers may process data on servers outside your country. Where we transfer information across borders, we take steps to make sure it stays protected in line with applicable law.

08How long we keep it

We keep registration information for up to 12 months after the Summit, which allows us to run the Summit, follow up with you as you have agreed, and meet legal or reporting requirements. After that we delete or anonymise it. If you withdraw or ask us to delete your details sooner, we will do so unless the law requires us to keep them.

09Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access limited to authorised people and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to: give you a copy of your information; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; and withdraw your consent to event communication at any time. You can also unsubscribe from any update we send. To exercise a right, contact us as set out at the end of this page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11Children

The Summit and this website are intended for senior professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Summit develops. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Questions about this page? Contact the Summit organising team by email at cipsat2027@gmail.com.
