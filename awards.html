CIPS India Awards 2027

Recognising Excellence. Celebrating Leadership. Inspiring the Future.

The CIPS India Awards 2027, held in conjunction with the CIPS International Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027, recognise outstanding individuals, teams and organisations driving excellence in procurement and supply chain management.

Across 12 award categories, the awards celebrate exceptional leadership, innovation, sustainability, digital transformation and measurable contributions to the profession.

Individual Awards · 01–04

Celebrating Outstanding Procurement Professionals & Leaders

01

Procurement Excellence Award

Recognises procurement professionals who demonstrate outstanding performance, commercial expertise and measurable contributions to organisational success.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Minimum three years of procurement or supply chain experience; professionals working below head-of-function level.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Measurable savings, cost avoidance, efficiency or quality improvements.
  • Excellence in sourcing, negotiation, contracting and supplier management.
  • Ethical procurement practices and compliance.
  • Positive influence on stakeholders and colleagues.

Supporting Evidence

One or two quantified case studies and a line-manager endorsement.

Individual₹25,000 + GST
02

Procurement Leader of the Year

Honours procurement leaders who have delivered exceptional strategic, financial or organisational impact while developing high-performing teams.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Department Heads, General Managers, Directors or equivalent leaders, excluding CPOs.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Significant financial, cultural or reputational impact.
  • Advancement of organisational procurement strategy.
  • Increased visibility and influence of the procurement function.
  • Team development, talent management and succession planning.

Supporting Evidence

Quantified strategic achievements and two testimonials (senior executive and team member).

Individual₹25,000 + GST
03

Emerging Procurement Leader

Celebrates rising procurement professionals demonstrating exceptional achievements, initiative and leadership potential early in their careers.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Age 30 or below as of 1 January 2027, or fewer than five years of procurement experience (final rule to be confirmed).

Evaluation Criteria

  • Achievements exceeding expectations for career stage.
  • Initiative, collaboration and leadership potential.
  • Progress in professional qualifications and development.
  • Contributions through mentoring, volunteering or knowledge sharing.

Supporting Evidence

Professional achievements and supporting testimonials, where available.

Individual₹25,000 + GST
04

CPO of the Year

Recognises senior procurement executives who have positioned procurement as a strategic driver of enterprise value, transformation and business resilience.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

CPO, Head of Procurement or equivalent, reporting to the CEO, CFO or COO, with at least 12 months in the role.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Procurement’s strategic influence at board level.
  • Enterprise-wide value creation, risk management and ESG outcomes.
  • Functional transformation through digitalisation and talent development.
  • Contributions to India’s procurement ecosystem.

Supporting Evidence

CEO/board endorsement and two to three quantified enterprise-level achievements.

Individual₹25,000 + GST
Company, Team & Organisation Awards · 01–08

Recognising Innovation, Performance & Organisational Impact

01

Best Procurement Team (Large Organization)

Recognises procurement teams delivering exceptional business performance, organisational influence and measurable value.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Proposed annual turnover of ₹1,000 crore or more (subject to confirmation).

Evaluation Criteria

  • Strategic influence of the procurement function.
  • Performance and organisational culture improvements.
  • Measurable savings, efficiency and competitive advantage.
  • Team capability and employee engagement.
Company₹50,000 + GST
02

Best Procurement Team (Small Organization)

Celebrates procurement teams delivering outstanding results through innovation, efficiency and effective resource utilisation.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Proposed fewer than 250 employees and/or annual turnover below ₹1,000 crore (subject to confirmation).

Evaluation Criteria

  • Procurement’s organisational contribution and influence.
  • Improvements in performance and team culture.
  • Measurable business value relative to available resources.
  • Team development and operational efficiency.
Company₹50,000 + GST
03

Best Sustainability Project / Program

Recognises procurement initiatives delivering measurable environmental, social and governance impact.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Procurement-led or organisation-led projects with demonstrated procurement involvement.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Clear and achievable ESG objectives.
  • Measurable outcomes in emissions, waste, water or responsible sourcing.
  • Supplier engagement and capability development.
  • Contributions to sustainability, human rights and social value.
Company₹50,000 + GST
04

Best Digital Transformation Project

Recognises procurement teams successfully implementing AI, automation or digital solutions to improve business performance.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

In-house procurement or supply chain teams.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Clear business challenge and technology-led solution.
  • Successful adoption and change management.
  • Measurable improvements in efficiency, savings, compliance or data quality.
  • Procurement leadership and solution scalability.
Company₹50,000 + GST
05

Public Procurement Projects

Celebrates public procurement initiatives demonstrating transparency, accountability and measurable improvements in public-service delivery.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Government departments, PSUs, autonomous bodies and eligible GeM-based procurement projects.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Compliance with public procurement rules and policies.
  • Value for money and improved citizen or service outcomes.
  • Transparency, fairness and audit readiness.
  • Replicability across public institutions.
Company₹50,000 + GST
06

Best Knowledge Infusion

Recognises learning and development initiatives that strengthen procurement capabilities and professional excellence.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Organisations, training institutions or consortia delivering structured procurement learning programmes.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Development of procurement and leadership capabilities.
  • Measurable improvement in organisational performance.
  • Training reach and professional qualifications achieved.
  • Mentoring, knowledge sharing and industry contribution.
Company₹50,000 + GST
07

Best Technology Partner

Recognises outstanding partnerships between technology providers and procurement teams that deliver measurable business improvements through successful technology implementation.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Technology/SaaS providers or implementation partners with a named client organisation. Joint entries with client procurement teams are encouraged.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Quantifiable client business outcomes.
  • Partnership quality, collaboration and implementation support.
  • Technology adoption and user experience.
  • Client satisfaction and successful delivery.

Supporting Evidence

Mandatory client testimonial.

Company₹50,000 + GST
08

Best Procurement Solution

Recognises innovative procurement products, platforms or services demonstrating proven market impact across multiple organisations.

Eligibility & criteria

Eligibility

Solution providers with live clients in India.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Innovation and differentiation from existing solutions.
  • Measurable results across at least two client organisations.
  • Relevance to Indian procurement requirements.
  • Data security, protection and future development roadmap.
Company₹50,000 + GST

Understanding the Difference Between Categories 07 & 08

07Best Technology Partner

Recognises the success of a specific partnership between a technology provider and a client organisation, focusing on collaboration, implementation and measurable outcomes.

08Best Procurement Solution

Recognises the broader innovation and market impact of a procurement product, platform or service, supported by proven results across multiple client organisations.

Before You Apply

General Nomination Guidelines

Participation

Open to CIPS members and non-members.

Eligibility Period

Completed work with measurable results, proposed within the previous 12–24 months.

Geographic Scope

Nominees or projects based in or primarily serving India (subject to confirmation).

Evidence

Quantified achievements, case studies and supporting testimonials where required.

Individual Entries

Each nominee may enter only one individual award category.

Attachments

Individual headshot or team photograph and supporting PDF or image files. Photos are resized automatically.

Client Disclosure

Mandatory for consultancy and technology-provider nominations.

Confidentiality

Entries accessible only to the authorised awards team and judges.

Judging

Proposed independent MCIPS/FCIPS judging panel, with conflicts of interest declared. Judges’ decisions are final, with no correspondence on shortlisting or winners.
