Cancellation Policy

What happens if you can no longer attend, and what happens if the Summit changes.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseCancellation Policy

01Registering your interest

The registration form on this website records your interest in attending. Our team then reviews your details and confirms your participation. This website itself does not take payments.

02If you can no longer attend

  • Please tell us as early as you can, so that we can offer your place to another delegate. Contact the organising team by email at cipsat2027@gmail.com, quoting your name and organisation.
  • Once we receive your message, we will cancel your registration and confirm this to you.
  • If you are unable to attend, you may ask to nominate a colleague in your place. Substitutes are subject to our approval, because the Summit is designed for senior decision-makers.

03Fees and refunds

The registration fee of ₹10,000 per delegate (plus GST) is invoiced separately after your place is confirmed. If you cancel a confirmed place, the following schedule applies, counted from the date we receive your cancellation:

When you cancelRefundSubstitute delegate
30 days or more before the SummitFull refundFree of charge
15 to 29 days before the Summit50% refundFree of charge
Fewer than 15 days before the SummitNo refundUp to 7 days before, at our discretion
No-showNo refundNot available

Refunds are made to the original payer, normally within a reasonable period after we confirm your cancellation. If the conditions in your own confirmation differ from this schedule, your confirmation applies.

04If we change or cancel the Summit

We may need to change the date, venue, format, programme or speakers, or to reschedule or cancel the Summit, including for reasons outside our control such as safety concerns, government restrictions, severe weather, travel disruption or insufficient capacity. If that happens, we will contact you as soon as reasonably possible using the details you gave us and explain your options. If the Summit is rescheduled or materially changed, you may withdraw your registration. If we cancel the Summit, any fee you have paid will be refunded in full or, if you prefer, credited to a future edition.

05Changes to speakers and programme

Speaker line-ups and programme details can change. A change of speaker or session is not, by itself, a reason for cancellation.

06No-shows

If you have a confirmed place and do not attend without telling us, we may treat your registration as cancelled and any conditions in your confirmation will apply.

07Your information after cancelling

When you cancel we stop using your details for the Summit, except where we need to keep them for a legal reason. You can ask us to delete your registration details. See the Privacy Policy.

Questions about this page? Contact the Summit organising team by email at cipsat2027@gmail.com.
