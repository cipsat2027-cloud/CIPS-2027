CIPS International Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027

About the Summit

Where Global Procurement Leadership Comes Together

The CIPS International Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027 is a one-day leadership platform created for senior professionals navigating a rapidly changing global business environment.

The Forum

Not simply a conference — a curated forum

The summit will bring together CPOs, procurement directors, supply chain leaders, transformation heads and senior business decision-makers to explore the strategies, technologies and leadership priorities redefining the profession.

This is not simply a conference — it is a curated forum for senior-level conversation, knowledge exchange and peer connection.

The Summit’s Promise

Where global procurement leadership meets to shape what comes next.

A senior, international procurement leadership platform that brings together CIPS leadership, top CPOs and supply chain decision-makers to discuss the issues shaping the future of the profession — from AI and resilience to strategic value, responsible sourcing and future-ready leadership.
