About CIPS

Shaping the Global Standards of Procurement & Supply Chain Excellence

The Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply (CIPS) is the world’s largest professional body dedicated to procurement and supply chain management. Operating under a Royal Charter granted in 1992, CIPS represents a global network of over 70,000 members across more than 150 countries.

Who We Are

As the independent custodian of the procurement profession, CIPS sets internationally recognised standards for professional capability, ethical conduct and supply chain governance.

Bridging public and private sector interests, CIPS works to strengthen procurement practices, advance professional excellence and build transparent, sustainable and resilient global supply networks.

The CIPS Legacy

A Legacy of Advancing the Procurement Profession

From its origins in 1932 to its position as a globally recognised professional institution, CIPS has played a defining role in transforming procurement from an administrative function into a strategic driver of business value, organisational resilience and sustainable growth.

Key Milestones
  1. 1932

    The Beginning

    Founded in the United Kingdom as the Purchasing Officers Association (POA), establishing early professional standards for industrial purchasing during a period of global economic uncertainty.

  2. 1967

    Strengthening the Profession

    The formation of the Institute of Purchasing and Supply (IPS), through a merger with the Institute of Public Supplies, brought together commercial and public-sector procurement expertise.

  3. 1992

    Royal Charter Recognition

    Awarded a Royal Charter by Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II, recognising procurement as a chartered profession committed to professional excellence and public benefit.

  4. 2014

    A New Global Identity

    Renamed the Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply, reflecting the expanding role of procurement across end-to-end supply chain management and strengthening the recognition of Chartered Professional status.

  5. Today

    A Global Professional Authority

    CIPS continues to advance international procurement standards through its regional presence and professional networks across the United Kingdom, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, Australasia and the Americas.

Professional Recognition

The Global Benchmark for Procurement Excellence

CIPS professional designations are internationally recognised credentials that demonstrate expertise, strategic leadership and a commitment to ethical procurement practices.

Through its professional qualifications and membership framework, CIPS establishes benchmarks for procurement capability at different stages of a professional’s career.

MCIPS

Member of the Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply

The International Benchmark for Procurement Professionals

MCIPS is a globally recognised professional designation demonstrating advanced procurement expertise, commercial competence and the ability to manage complex supply chain responsibilities.

What MCIPS Represents

MCIPS reflects professional capability across key areas of procurement and supply management, including:

  • Strategic sourcing and category management
  • Commercial contracting and supplier relationships
  • Contractual and financial risk management
  • Sustainable and ethical procurement
  • Advanced negotiation and supply chain governance
Pathways to MCIPS

Professionals can pursue MCIPS through established qualification and assessment routes, including:

  • Examination Pathway: Completion of CIPS Level 4 Diploma, Level 5 Advanced Diploma and Level 6 Professional Diploma, supported by a minimum of three years of relevant professional experience.
  • Accredited Academic Pathway: Recognition of eligible accredited university qualifications.
  • Management Entry Route (MER): A structured assessment pathway for experienced senior procurement professionals.
Maintaining Chartered Professional Status

Eligible MCIPS members maintaining Chartered Procurement and Supply Professional status are required to complete 30 hours of Continuing Professional Development (CPD) annually and renew the CIPS Ethical Procurement and Supply certification each year.

FCIPS

Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply

Recognising Exceptional Leadership in Procurement

FCIPS represents the highest level of professional recognition within CIPS, acknowledging individuals who have demonstrated outstanding leadership, strategic influence and sustained contributions to the procurement and supply chain profession.

What FCIPS Represents

Fellowship recognises senior professionals who have made a significant impact through:

  • Executive leadership and strategic decision-making
  • Procurement and supply chain transformation
  • Contributions to professional standards and industry development
  • Mentorship and advancement of future procurement leaders
  • Influence on organisational, public-sector and global supply policy
Eligibility and Professional Standing

FCIPS is intended for experienced leaders, including Chief Procurement Officers, Global Supply Chain Directors, senior government procurement professionals and distinguished industry experts.

Candidates demonstrate sustained professional achievement, leadership impact and contributions to the profession through a formal assessment and peer-review process.

FCIPS members represent an influential community helping shape the future direction of procurement and global supply chain management.

The CIPS Qualification Pathway

A Structured Journey from Foundation to Global Leadership

CIPS provides a progressive qualification framework designed to develop professional capability from foundational procurement knowledge to advanced strategic leadership.

Stage 1 of 4 · Levels 2–3

Foundations & Tactical Procurement

2
Level 2 — Certificate in Procurement and Supply Operations

An introduction to fundamental procurement activities, including purchasing processes, purchase orders, administrative controls and supplier communication.

3
Level 3 — Advanced Certificate in Procurement and Supply Operations

Develops tactical procurement capabilities, including cost analysis, commercial agreements, inventory monitoring and supplier management.

Stage 2 of 4 · Levels 4–6

Professional & Strategic Development

4
Level 4 — Diploma in Procurement and Supply

Builds operational procurement competence through commercial contracting, sourcing methodologies, whole-life costing and negotiation strategies.

5
Level 5 — Advanced Diploma in Procurement and Supply

Develops management-level expertise in category management, contractual and financial risk, ethical supply practices and team leadership.

6
Level 6 — Professional Diploma in Procurement and Supply

Focuses on executive-level procurement capabilities, including corporate strategy, enterprise supply chain design, procurement transformation and strategic leadership.

Why CIPS Anchors Summit 2027

Connecting Global Expertise. Shaping the Future of Procurement.

The CIPS International Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027 brings together the global procurement community at a time when organisations are redefining how they source, collaborate, innovate and build resilience.

As global supply networks respond to geopolitical shifts, AI-driven transformation, supply chain disruptions, sustainability commitments and evolving trade corridors, procurement has emerged as a critical driver of enterprise strategy.

Anchored by CIPS’s professional expertise and global leadership network, the summit creates a platform for Chief Procurement Officers, MCIPS and FCIPS professionals, industry leaders and senior decision-makers to exchange insights, share practical experiences and explore the next generation of procurement practices.

Through strategic dialogue and collaboration, the summit aims to advance a shared vision of intelligent, resilient, transparent and ethically governed global supply chains.
